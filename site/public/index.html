Calm, private native Android software

Every shkriuss app is written in pure Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 13–17+, designed primarily for GrapheneOS with Exploit Protection Compatibility Mode off, zero Google Play services, zero WebViews, and zero trackers.

Pure Native Kotlin & Compose Zero GMS / Firebase Offline-First & E2EE Backups GPL-3.0-or-later
